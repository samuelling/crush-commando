Crush Commando×Hollow Dash

Two games stitched together by rifts. Walk into one and you come out in the other world, and your friends can run either one with you.

Rifts sit in the first mission of every Crush Commando world and in five Hollow Dash chapters. Press G (or tap Join up top) to jump straight to a friend, wherever they are. The ≡ button brings you back here.
